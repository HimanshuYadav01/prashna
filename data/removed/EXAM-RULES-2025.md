# Examination Rules for B.Tech Programmes, Version 2.2

Issued by the Controller of Examinations. Effective from 1 January 2025. To be read with the Academic Regulations (ACAD-REG-2024).

1.1 End-semester theory examinations are conducted at the close of each semester as per the date sheet notified by the Examination Section. Seating plans are published on the IMS notice board before each examination day.

1.2 Students must carry their university identity card and admit card to every examination. A student without both documents shall not be admitted to the examination hall.

1.3 Entry to the examination hall closes thirty minutes after the commencement of the examination. No student may leave the hall during the first sixty minutes.

2.1 Eligibility to appear in the end-semester examination is governed by the attendance requirement in the Academic Regulations and any circular revising it. Detained students are not admitted.

2.2 A student who is absent in an end-semester examination shall be marked ABSENT and may apply for the supplementary examination as per clause 8 of the Academic Regulations.

3.1 Use of mobile phones, smart watches or any unauthorised material in the examination hall constitutes Unfair Means (UFM). A UFM case is reported by the invigilator and decided by the UFM Committee.

3.2 Penalties for proven UFM range from cancellation of the paper concerned to cancellation of the entire semester's examinations, depending on the gravity of the case.

4.1 Answer scripts are evaluated centrally. Marks for internal assessment are displayed by the course coordinator before the end-semester examination.

4.2 A student may apply for re-evaluation of an end-semester answer script within seven working days of the declaration of results, on payment of the prescribed fee per course as per the Fee Schedule.

4.3 Re-evaluation is limited to two courses per semester. The re-evaluated mark, whether higher or lower, is final.

5.1 Results are declared on the IMS portal. Any discrepancy must be reported to the Examination Section within ten working days of declaration.
