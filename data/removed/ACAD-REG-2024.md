# Academic Regulations for B.Tech Programmes, Version 3.1

Issued by the Office of the Dean (Academics). Effective from 1 July 2024.

7.1 Attendance shall be computed course-wise as the percentage of classes attended out of classes held, calculated by the examination section at the end of the teaching period.

7.2 A student must have a minimum attendance of 75 percent in a course to be permitted to appear in the end-semester examination of that course. A student falling below this threshold shall be marked DETAINED in that course.

7.3 Attendance condonation on medical grounds may be granted by the Dean (Academics) up to a maximum of 10 percent, on production of certified medical records, subject to clause 7.2.

8.1 A student who fails a course or is marked absent in the end-semester examination may register for the supplementary examination in the following session.

8.2 Eligibility for the supplementary examination requires that the student's result in the course is FAIL or ABSENT. Students marked DETAINED are not eligible for the supplementary examination and must repeat the course.

8.3 Application for the supplementary examination shall be made through the examination portal within ten working days of the declaration of results, on payment of the prescribed fee per course.

9.1 The minimum total marks required to pass a course is 40 out of 100, where total marks are the sum of internal assessment and the end-semester examination marks.

9.2 A student securing less than the minimum total marks shall be assigned the result FAIL in that course.

10.1 Credits for a course are awarded only on securing the result PASS.
