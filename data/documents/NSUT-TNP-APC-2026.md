1 The Training and Placement Section, NSUT, invites applications from students of the B.Tech 2030 graduating batch for the role of Volunteers, and from the B.Tech 2029 graduating batch for Assistant Placement Coordinator (APC).

2 Volunteers and Assistant Placement Coordinators play an important role in supporting the Training and Placement Cell in facilitating internship and full-time recruitment opportunities for students. They work closely with Placement Coordinators and company representatives to ensure the smooth and efficient execution of placement activities.

3 The above roles provide students with valuable exposure to coordination, communication, problem-solving and organizational management. Students also get opportunities to interact with alumni, senior students and recruiters during campus recruitment drives, gaining practical experience in handling real-time placement operations.
