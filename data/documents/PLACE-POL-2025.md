# Placement Policy, Training and Placement Cell

Issued by the Training and Placement Cell with the approval of the Registrar. Version 2.0. Effective from 10 January 2025.

3.1 A student must have a Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of at least 6.0 at the time of registration to be eligible to register for campus placements.

3.2 A student must have no active backlogs at the time of registration for campus placements. A backlog is active until the course result is PASS.

3.3 Students may register for the placement season through the placement portal during the registration window announced by the Training and Placement Cell.

4.1 A student who accepts an offer is deemed placed and may appear for further processes only as permitted by the one-offer policy announced for the season.

5.1 Eligibility disputes shall be decided by the Training and Placement Officer, whose decision shall be final.
