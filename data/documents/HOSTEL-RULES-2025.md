# Hostel Rules and Allotment Policy, Version 1.3

Issued by the Office of the Chief Warden. Effective from 1 August 2025.

1.1 Hostel accommodation is available to full-time students of the university, subject to availability of seats. Allotment is made for one academic year at a time and does not confer a right to re-allotment.

1.2 Allotment priority is: (a) outstation first-year students by admission rank; (b) continuing residents in good standing; (c) other outstation students by distance from Delhi; (d) remaining applicants by CGPA.

1.3 Applications for hostel allotment are made through the hostel portal within the window notified by the Chief Warden at the start of each academic year.

2.1 Hostel fees comprise seat rent and mess advance as notified in the Fee Schedule. Fees are payable for the full semester at the time of allotment or renewal.

2.2 A resident who vacates within the first thirty days of the semester is eligible for a refund of seat rent on a pro-rata basis; the mess advance is adjusted against actual mess dues. No seat-rent refund is admissible after thirty days.

3.1 Mess services are compulsory for all residents. Mess rebate for absence is admissible only for a continuous absence of seven days or more, applied for in advance to the mess committee.

4.1 Residents must mark biometric attendance daily. The hostel gates close at 11:00 PM; late entry requires prior written permission of the warden.

4.2 Guests are not permitted in residential blocks. Day visitors may be received in the common room between 9:00 AM and 7:00 PM with an entry in the visitors register.

5.1 Ragging in any form is an offence under law and university regulations and leads to expulsion from the hostel and rustication proceedings.

5.2 Consumption or possession of alcohol, tobacco or narcotics in the hostel premises leads to immediate expulsion from the hostel.

6.1 A resident with more than two active backlogs at the time of renewal may be denied re-allotment, at the discretion of the Chief Warden.
