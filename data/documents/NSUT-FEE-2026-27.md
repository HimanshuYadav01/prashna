# Annual Fee of all Programs for Students Taking Admission in Academic Session 2026-27

Netaji Subhas University of Technology. Notification F.No. 220(558)/AnnualFeeStructure/2025/Acad/NSUT/147, dated 14/05/2026. Transcribed (OCR) from the scanned original notification; B.Tech-relevant sections. Original PDF retained in data/real_docs.

1.1 Fees for Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) and Bachelor of Design (B.Des) students admitted in Academic Session 2026-27, per annum. Government component: Tuition Fee Rs. 1,08,000 (2026-27), Rs. 1,13,000 (2027-28), Rs. 1,28,000 (2028-29), Rs. 1,33,000 (2029-30). University Fee Rs. 82,000 (2026-27), Rs. 86,500 (2027-28), Rs. 92,000 (2028-29), Rs. 95,000 (2029-30).

1.2 Non-government component per annum (2026-27): Student Fund Rs. 27,200 (co-curricular activities, training and placement, extra-curricular activities, annual gathering, students welfare, institutional development and related heads); Facilities and Services Charges Rs. 25,800; Chhatra Vittiya Sahayta Evam Protsaahan Kosh (CVSPK) Rs. 10,000; Alumni Fund Rs. 1,000 (first year only); Fund for Incubation/Entrepreneurship/Startup Activities Rs. 1,000; Examination Fee Rs. 15,000.

1.3 Total annual fee for B.Tech: Rs. 2,70,000 (2026-27); Rs. 2,90,000 (2027-28); Rs. 3,19,000 (2028-29); Rs. 3,33,000 (2029-30). Fee (if any) for MOOCs/NPTEL courses shall be borne by the student.

13.1 Course Re-Registration / Summer Semester fees: fee for UG/PG courses during a Regular Semester is Rs. 7,000 per subject.

13.2 Fee for Summer Semester for UG/PG: Exam-only mode Rs. 10,000 per subject; Study mode Rs. 14,000 per subject. Fee for MOOCs (NPTEL courses): nil.

14.1 University Annual Fee Policy: students permitted semester/year withdrawal pay the fees for the year of withdrawal, and are not charged again when completing the year against the withdrawal year.

14.2 Students not promoted to the next academic year for want of required credits are not required to deposit annual fees, but shall pay course re-registration fees for the courses they study to earn the requisite credits for promotion.

14.3 Complete withdrawal (2nd year onwards) intimated before the last date of course-registration approval of the odd semester, with no classes attended, is eligible for refund of fees after deduction of Rs. 5,000 processing charges; withdrawal intimated after that date receives no refund of university fees. First-year cases are governed by the University Admission Fee refund policy.

14.4 The schedule of fee submission is notified by the Academic Section before each academic year begins.
