# Fee Schedule for B.Tech Programmes, Academic Year 2026-27

Issued by the Accounts Section with the approval of the Registrar. Notice No. ACCT/2026/14. Effective from 1 July 2026.

1.1 The tuition fee for all B.Tech programmes is Rs. 95,000 per semester for students admitted in the 2023 batch and later.

1.2 In addition to tuition, every student shall pay per semester: a development fee of Rs. 10,000, a student welfare fund contribution of Rs. 2,500, and an examination fee of Rs. 2,000. The total payable per semester is Rs. 1,09,500.

1.3 One-time charges at admission are: institute security deposit (refundable) Rs. 10,000 and admission processing fee Rs. 5,000.

2.1 The fee for each supplementary examination is Rs. 800 per course, payable through the examination portal at the time of application.

2.2 The fee for re-evaluation of an answer script is Rs. 1,000 per course.

3.1 Semester fees must be paid within fifteen days of the start of the semester. A late payment fine of Rs. 100 per day applies thereafter, up to a maximum of Rs. 5,000.

3.2 A student whose fees remain unpaid thirty days after the start of the semester shall have registration withheld and shall not be permitted to appear in examinations until dues are cleared.

4.1 Hostel fees are separate and are notified by the Chief Warden. For 2026-27 the hostel seat rent is Rs. 25,000 per semester and the mess advance is Rs. 20,000 per semester.

5.1 Fee concessions and reimbursements under government scholarship schemes (including Post Matric Scholarships via the National Scholarship Portal) are governed by the applicable scheme guidelines; students must still meet payment deadlines unless an official deferment is granted by the Accounts Section.
