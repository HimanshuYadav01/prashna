# Circular: Revision of Minimum Attendance Requirement

Issued by the Office of the Dean (Academics), Circular No. ACAD/2026/08. Effective from 1 August 2026. This circular supersedes clause 11.2 of the Regulations for Undergraduate Programme of Bachelor of Technology (NSUT-REG-BTECH-2019). SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT, marked synthetic in the Source Register.

1 With effect from 1 August 2026, the minimum attendance required in a subject to be eligible to appear in the MSE/ESE of that subject is revised from 75 percent to 80 percent of the total number of classes including lectures, tutorials and practicals held till the MSE/ESE.

2 All other provisions of the Regulations, including relaxation by the Dean Academics under clauses 11.3 to 11.5 and the absolute floor under clause 11.6, remain unchanged and continue to apply with reference to the revised threshold.

3 This revision applies to all programmes and all batches from the date of effect. Attendance computed for examination sessions prior to the date of effect shall continue to be governed by the earlier threshold of 75 percent.
