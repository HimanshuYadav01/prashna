# Circular: Revision of Minimum Attendance Requirement

Issued by the Office of the Dean (Academics), Circular No. ACAD/2026/08. Effective from 1 August 2026. This circular supersedes clause 7.2 of the Academic Regulations for B.Tech Programmes (ACAD-REG-2024).

1 With effect from 1 August 2026, the minimum attendance required in a course to be permitted to appear in the end-semester examination of that course is revised from 75 percent to 80 percent.

2 All other provisions of the Academic Regulations, including condonation under clause 7.3, remain unchanged and continue to apply subject to the revised threshold.

3 This revision applies to all programmes and all batches from the date of effect. Attendance computed for examination sessions prior to the date of effect shall continue to be governed by the earlier threshold.
