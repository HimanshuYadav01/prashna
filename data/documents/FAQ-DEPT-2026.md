# Department Help-Desk FAQ (Computer Science)

Maintained by student volunteers of the CSE department help desk. Last updated 15 September 2026. This page is informational and not an official notification.

1 What attendance do I need for end-semester exams? As per our understanding 65 percent is enough in most cases, and the department is usually lenient about it.

2 How do I apply for a supplementary exam? Apply on the examination portal after results are declared and pay the fee for each course.

3 Who do I contact about attendance issues? The course coordinator first, then the department office.
